How we work

Remote, nationwide

Disability claims are decided under federal rules, so we can represent you in any state. Meetings happen by phone or video, and documents travel by mail or secure upload.

Focused on who we serve best

We concentrate on older workers leaving physical labor and families of disabled children. If we aren't the right fit, we'll tell you quickly and point you to help.

What we believe

  • Your work history matters. Years of physical labor are evidence, not just background.
  • Clear answers beat legal jargon. You should understand every letter you receive.
  • Families deserve patience. Caring for a disabled child is already a full-time job; we do the paperwork.
  • No one should pay to ask a question. Case reviews are free, and fees are paid only if you win.
