What we handle

SSDI: Social Security Disability Insurance

For people who worked and paid Social Security taxes, then became unable to work because of a medical condition expected to last at least a year or result in death.

SSI: Supplemental Security Income

A needs-based program for disabled adults and children with limited income and resources. It does not depend on a work record.
