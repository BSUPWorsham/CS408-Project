Who we help

Workers 50 and older

Years of lifting, driving, building, or caring for others can leave you unable to keep going. Age and work history change how Social Security weighs your claim, and we know how to present that.

Families of disabled children

Whether your child is a minor or a disabled adult, SSI can provide steady support. We help families gather records and move through each stage.

Simple steps, no phone tag

Our online intake asks a few plain questions at your own pace. If we can help, we save your answers and give you a summary you can download. If we are not the right fit, we point you to organizations that may be.
